What we check

DNS & email authentication

DNSSEC, SPF, DKIM and DMARC — the controls that decide whether someone can forge mail from your domain.

TLS & certificates

Certificate validity, expiry, protocol configuration and weakness that enables interception.

HTTP security headers

CSP, HSTS, and related headers that limit the damage of a compromised page or third-party script.

Exposed services

Publicly reachable hosts and ports, plus subdomains that leak through certificate transparency.

Everything is gathered from public sources. We do not exploit, brute force, or touch internal systems.
